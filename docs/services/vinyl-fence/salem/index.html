QUESTIONS WE HEAR EVERY WEEK

How much does a vinyl fence cost?

Vinyl privacy fences typically run $45-$65 per linear foot installed. It costs more than cedar up front and less over its life, since there is nothing to stain or replace.

Does vinyl fencing hold up in the rain?

Yes. Vinyl does not absorb water, so it cannot rot or grow mold the way untreated wood can. A rinse with a hose keeps it clean.

Can vinyl fence panels be installed on a slope?

Yes. We rack or step panels to follow the grade so the fence looks intentional rather than gapped at the bottom.
