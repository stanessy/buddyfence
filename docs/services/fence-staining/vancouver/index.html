QUESTIONS WE HEAR EVERY WEEK

How often should a cedar fence be stained?

Every 2 to 4 years in the Pacific Northwest, depending on sun exposure. North-facing fences that stay damp benefit from the shorter end of that range.

Should I stain a new cedar fence right away?

Wait 4 to 8 weeks for the wood to dry, then seal it. Staining wet cedar traps moisture and the stain will not penetrate.
